# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Car wash owners and managers who need a single hub to run their business — managing services, packages, customer inquiries, scheduling, and portfolio showcases.

## Product Purpose

An all-in-one admin dashboard that gives car wash business owners complete control over their operations: from defining services and packages, to handling customer inquiries, managing the calendar, and showcasing their portfolio. Success means the owner can run the entire business from one place without juggling multiple tools.

## Positioning

All-in-one car wash management — services, scheduling, inquiries, and portfolio — everything in one dashboard.

## Brand Personality

Friendly and approachable. The interface should feel warm, helpful, and personal — like a trusted business partner rather than cold enterprise software. The brand voice is straightforward and human, not corporate or technical.

## Anti-references

Cluttered, overly complex dashboards. The admin should avoid busy layouts, dense data dumps, and visual noise. Every screen puts the task first. Anti-references include enterprise tools like Jira or Salesforce that overwhelm with chrome and configuration.

## Design Principles

- **Approachable clarity**: Every screen leads with the task, not the chrome. Interfaces explain themselves through clear hierarchy and plain language.
- **Practice what you preach**: The admin should reflect the same premium, professional quality that Ryan's Autoreiniging delivers to its customers.
- **Show, don't tell**: Surface key information — stats, statuses, upcoming events — at a glance without requiring extra clicks.
- **Confidence through consistency**: Uniform patterns for tables, forms, and actions so the owner never second-guesses where to find something.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard web accessibility through sensible semantic HTML and keyboard navigation. No specific WCAG level or special accommodations required beyond baseline best practices.
